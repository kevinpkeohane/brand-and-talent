What we do

Four connected areas of work.

Each engagement is built around your specific challenge. Most briefs touch more than one area—and the connection between them is where the real value sits.

Brand positioning and narrative

Make the company easier to understand, remember and choose.

  • Market, customer and competitor diagnosis
  • Leadership interviews and facilitated alignment
  • Positioning territory and strategic choice
  • Value proposition and brand idea
  • Messaging architecture and narrative
  • Sales story, pitch language and message tools

Typical result: a distinctive position and one coherent story that guides marketing, sales and leadership communication.

Culture and EX to CX alignment

Make the organization capable of delivering what the brand promises.

  • Culture and employee-experience diagnosis
  • EX friction mapped to customer-experience consequences
  • Mission, vision, values and behavior systems
  • Employer value proposition and talent narrative
  • Leadership alignment and engagement workshops
  • Activation roadmap, tools and measurement

Typical result: clearer priorities, more useful behaviors and less internal friction between the promise and the experience.

Go-to-market and growth activation

Turn a clear position into focused commercial action.

  • Audience prioritization and personas
  • Campaign strategy and message development
  • Sales enablement and playbooks
  • Demand-generation architecture
  • Alliance and partner activation
  • Content systems and measurement logic

Typical result: a focused GTM system in which story, audience, channels and sales activity reinforce one another.

AI-enabled delivery

Use AI to accelerate the right strategy—not to manufacture one.

  • AI-assisted research and synthesis
  • Brand Guardian systems for consistent content
  • Agentic content and workflow automation
  • Faster production of on-brand GTM assets
  • Human review, judgment and governance

Typical result: shorter strategy-to-market cycles and more consistent activation without surrendering strategic judgment.

How an engagement works

A clear logic, shaped around your challenge.

Every engagement moves through the same underlying process—from defining the real problem to activating the answer.

Stage What happens What you gain
Stage 1Clarify Focused discovery identifies the real constraint, decision and stakeholders. A shared definition of the problem and the right scope.
Stage 2Discover Research, interviews and evidence reveal what customers, employees, leaders and competitors are telling us. Insight grounded in the reality of the market and organization.
Stage 3Decide together Leadership working sessions turn evidence into choices and a unifying direction. Alignment, ownership and a strategy leaders can explain.
Stage 4Build The position becomes a narrative, message system, culture tools and activation plan. Practical assets people can use—not just a strategy deck.
Stage 5Activate A senior team supports rollout across the moments that matter. Momentum, consistency and measurable progress.

Engagement formats

Start with the problem, not a predefined package.

A first conversation will identify what needs solving now, what can wait, and the smallest useful engagement that can create movement.

Best for: a defined issue needing independent challenge

Decision clarity session

Focused preparation, a facilitated working session and a concise action summary. The right starting point when there is a specific decision to make.

Best for: a specific positioning, narrative or GTM question

Strategy sprint

Discovery, working sessions, strategic recommendation and core tools. Designed for rapid leadership alignment on a focused challenge.

Best for: a connected brand, culture and growth challenge

Bespoke transformation

Phased senior-team engagement from diagnosis through rollout. For multi-stakeholder challenges with significant activation needs.

Best for: teams needing a fast, affordable GTM foundation
