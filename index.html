Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

What a brand, go-to-market, and growth engagement with Brand & Talent looks like — and how the assembled-team model works.

What does a brand and go-to-market (GTM) consultancy actually do?

A brand and go-to-market consultancy finds the single true story inside your business and turns it into growth — brand positioning and messaging strategy, a go-to-market and demand-generation system, and the sales-enablement infrastructure that converts narrative into qualified pipeline. Brand & Talent covers both the brand strategy and the operating system that runs it, including AI-enabled delivery.

How is the “assembled senior team” model different from a traditional agency?

Instead of a fixed agency roster, we assemble a bespoke team of senior specialists — writers, designers, creatives, strategists, and technologists — matched to your specific brief. You get past masters only, one accountable strategic lead, and no junior overhead or house style to defend. It's senior brand and GTM expertise without agency bloat.

What is the Go-to-Market Maven™ playbook?

The Go-to-Market Maven™ is our productized go-to-market playbook. It pairs AI-powered market analysis with expert human strategists to deliver Fortune 500-level campaign strategy — positioning, a single-minded story, and custom buyer personas — in days rather than weeks, at startup-friendly prices.

What is EX→CX and why does it matter for growth?

EX→CX (our EX2CX methodology) connects employee-experience friction to customer-experience outcomes, treating culture as a measurable commercial lever rather than an HR exercise. Aligning employer brand, culture, and customer experience removes the internal drag that quietly undermines go-to-market performance.

How do you use agentic AI in brand and GTM delivery?

We build the agentic-AI content and automation infrastructure ourselves — systems like Brand Guardian AI and the Go-to-Market Maven™ engine — compressing research, analysis, and on-brand production by up to 160× while keeping every asset on tone of voice and brand message. AI-native isn't a slide in our deck; it's how the work ships.

Who do you work with, and where are you based?

Brand & Talent works with startups, in-house teams, agencies, and enterprises across the US and EU, remotely, from Spokane, Washington. Engagements range from fixed-scope Go-to-Market Maven™ playbooks to full bespoke brand, GTM, growth, and EX→CX programs led by founder Kevin Keohane.
